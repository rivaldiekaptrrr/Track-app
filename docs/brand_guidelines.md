## 1. Core Brand Concept & Narrative

**TrackIt** is a 2-in-1 ecosystem harmonizing **Daily Expense Tracking & Financial Control** with **Comprehensive Wedding Planning & Milestone Budgeting**.

```
             ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
             │       INFINITY RIBBON & WEDDING KNOT (∞)     │
             │   Eternal Matrimonial Unity · Perpetual Flow │
             └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                    │ (Interlocking Form)
             ┌──────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
             │            DUAL TRACKING STEMS (II)          │
             │   Pillar 1: Expense Tracking & Cashflow      │
             │   Pillar 2: Wedding Budget & Milestone Plan  │
             └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Top Infinity Ribbon (∞)**: A continuous 3D twisting ribbon forming an eternal wedding bowknot and financial cycle.
- **Monogram 'T' Structure**: Forms the iconic initial of **TrackIt** with high recognizability at all scales.
- **Dual Vertical Track Stems**: Two parallel rails (with a 45° beveled bottom slice) representing the dual-engine architecture of the app (Expense + Wedding).
- **Cobalt to Aqua Gradient**: Symbolizes the transition from bank-grade financial security to modern, delightful milestone planning.

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing Rules

- **Clear Space:** Maintain a minimum exclusion zone of **`0.5X`** (where `X` is the height of the top infinity ribbon loop) around the mark.
- **Minimum Digital Sizing:**
  - Full Horizontal Lockup: Minimum `120px` width.
  - Standalone Mark: Minimum `24px` width.
  - Micro Sizes (16px favicon): Use `trackit-symbol-small.svg` or `favicon.ico`.

---

## 5. Typography

- **Brand Wordmark:** Geometric Sans-Serif (**Plus Jakarta Sans** / **Inter** Bold/ExtraBold, 800 weight, `-0.02em` tracking).
- **App UI Font:** Inter / System Default Sans.
